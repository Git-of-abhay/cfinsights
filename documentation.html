01 · Start

Getting started

  1. Enter one public Codeforces handle, or choose an example chip.
  2. Optionally enable Compare Mode and add a second handle.
  3. Select Analyze Profiles and wait while public API data loads.
  4. Use the dashboard tabs to move between summary, training, and diagnostic views.
CFinsights does not require a login, Codeforces password, or API key.

02 · Read

Overview

Rating historyProgress across rated contests, including rank and rating change.
Difficulty distributionUnique solved problems grouped by rating bands.
Submission overviewAccepted submissions, total attempts, verdict mix, and languages.
Recent contestsYour latest rated appearances with direct contest context.

Use Overview for a fast health check. Treat rating, solve breadth, and submission efficiency as complementary signals.

03 · Patterns

Analytics and activity heatmap

Analytics highlights topic peaks, earned badges, daily submission activity, and weekly consistency. The heatmap represents the last 365 days. Darker cells mean more submissions.

Interactive heatmap controls

Choose 30d, 90d, or 1y to focus the period while keeping the year visible for context. Select or keyboard-focus any cell to read its date and submission count.

How to interpret it

Look for sustainable weekly rhythm rather than isolated high-volume days. Long blank bands usually reveal a consistency problem; dense clusters followed by silence may indicate burnout or contest-only practice.

04 · Act

Practice

The Practice tab converts your history into next actions. Undefeated Problems are problems with repeated failed attempts and no accepted submission. Recent Unsolved Problems show fresher gaps.

Start with a problem near your current comfort band, time-box the fresh attempt, then read an editorial if the same approach fails repeatedly. Return later and solve it without the editorial to confirm retention.

05 · Diagnose

Insights

The Time Wasted Index estimates time spent across recent failed chains. The Failure Dashboard groups errors by topic, verdict, and rating. Use the intersection—such as repeated wrong answers in dynamic programming—to choose a focused practice block.

The metrics are coaching signals derived from public submissions. They are not a judgment of ability.

06 · Compare

Profile comparison

Compare Mode loads two profiles and aligns ratings, solve counts, verdicts, topics, and progress. Use it to understand different strengths or learning paths. Raw totals can reflect account age, so compare rates and progression alongside totals.

07 · Score

CFinsights profile score

The score combines eleven profile metrics and adjusts their weights by rating tier. This keeps the focus appropriate: early tiers reward fundamentals and consistency, while advanced tiers place more emphasis on contest performance and difficult solves.

Total score = sum of each metric score multiplied by its tier weight. Open “View Calculation Breakdown” on a profile to see the exact contribution. The star rating is the total score divided by 20.

08 · Learn

Guided tutor

Select the fixed Tutor button to start a contextual walkthrough. Before analysis it explains Search and Compare. After a profile loads, restart it to tour every dashboard tab and highlight the relevant panel. Press ? to open it and Esc to close it.

09 · Trust

Data and privacy

Profile, rating, contest, blog, and submission data comes directly from the public Codeforces API. The app stores your light/dark theme preference and tutor completion state in local browser storage. It includes no login flow. Google Analytics measures page views and interface actions such as opening Tutor or selecting a dashboard tab with measurement ID G-3RY0H6WN3E; Codeforces handles are not included in custom events, and IP anonymization is requested in its configuration.

10 · Help

Troubleshooting

Handle not found

Check spelling and remove spaces. Handles are public identifiers.

Too many requests

Codeforces rate limits repeated API calls. Wait briefly, then use Try Again.

Some panels are empty

New or inactive profiles may not have rating, contest, or submission history for every metric.

Charts appear compressed

Refresh after resizing the browser. The charts recalculate against their container width.
